First release.

I hunt bugs. Most of what I learn evaporates the moment I close the tab, so this is the
place where it stops evaporating.

Three kinds of posts will land here:

- **Writeups** — redacted, program name withheld unless disclosure is granted. The bug
  mechanics, not the brag.
- **Methodology** — how I pick targets, how I run recon, what I stopped doing and why.
- **Tooling** — the scripts I keep rewriting until they stop annoying me.

No comments, no analytics, no newsletter. Just posts.
